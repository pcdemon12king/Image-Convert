Contact ImageConvert

Have a question, found a bug, or want to suggest a new feature or format? We'd like to hear from you. Use the email below and we'll get back to you as soon as possible.

Please include as much detail as possible — for example, which format you were converting from and to, what browser you used, and what happened. That helps us reproduce and fix issues faster.

What We Can Help With

Bug reports, format support requests, general questions about how the converter works, and feedback about the design or usability of the site.

What We Cannot Help With

We cannot recover lost files, repair corrupted images or videos, or provide technical support for third-party software. ImageConvert processes files locally in your browser, so we never have access to your files.

Response Time

This is an independent project, so replies may take a few days. We read every message, even if we can't always respond immediately.
