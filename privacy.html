Last updated: January 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how ImageConvert ("we", "us", or "our") handles information when you visit and use this website.

ImageConvert performs image and video conversion directly in your browser using client-side technologies (WebAssembly). Your files are not uploaded to, stored on, or transmitted to our servers. We do not have access to the content of the files you convert.

Like most websites, we may collect non-personal information automatically through analytics and advertising tools. This may include your browser type, device type, approximate location (country or region level), pages visited, and time spent on the site. This information is aggregated and does not personally identify you.

This website may use cookies and similar technologies to improve your experience and to display relevant advertising. Cookies are small text files stored on your device by your browser. You can disable cookies in your browser settings at any time, though some features of the site may not function properly as a result.

Users may opt out of personalized advertising by visiting Google Ads Settings. Alternatively, users can opt out of some third-party vendors' use of cookies for personalized advertising by visiting www.aboutads.info.

5. Analytics

We may use third-party analytics services to understand how visitors use the site. These services may collect information sent by your browser as part of a web page request, such as cookies or your IP address. The data collected is used in aggregate form to improve the site.

6. Children's Privacy

This website is not directed at children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information on this site, please contact us so we can remove it.

7. Your Rights

Depending on your location, you may have the right to access, correct, or delete personal information we hold about you. Since we do not collect personal information directly, most such requests can be handled through your browser settings and Google Ads Settings linked above.

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy, please visit the Contact page.
