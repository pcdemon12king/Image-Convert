About ImageConvert

ImageConvert is a free, browser-based tool for converting images and videos between popular formats. It was built with a simple idea in mind: converting a file should not require installing software, creating an account, or uploading your personal files to a stranger's server.

Why ImageConvert Exists

Most online converters work the same way. You upload your file to a remote server, wait for it to be processed, and then download the result. That approach has two problems. First, it means your private photos and videos leave your device and sit on someone else's infrastructure. Second, it often comes with file size limits, watermarks, daily caps, or forced sign-ups.

ImageConvert takes a different approach. All conversion happens directly inside your browser using modern web technologies. Your files never leave your device. There is no upload step, no queue, and no server receiving your data.

How It Works Under the Hood

For image conversion, ImageConvert uses wasm-vips — a WebAssembly build of the libvips image processing library. This lets the browser handle formats like JPG, PNG, WebP, GIF, BMP, TIFF, AVIF and ICO with the same engine trusted by professional image pipelines.

For video conversion, ImageConvert uses FFmpeg compiled to WebAssembly. FFmpeg is the industry-standard multimedia framework used by countless applications and services. Running it in the browser means you can convert between MP4, WebM, MOV, AVI, MKV and GIF without installing anything.

What You Can Do With It

You can convert photos for the web, prepare images for a design project, change video formats for compatibility with a specific device, create GIFs from short clips, or simply shrink files by adjusting quality settings. The tool is designed to be straightforward: choose a file, pick an output format, adjust quality if needed, and download the result.

Privacy by Design

Because conversion happens locally in your browser, ImageConvert does not collect, store, or transmit your files. There is no backend processing queue, no cloud storage, and no account system. What you convert stays on your device.

Who Built This

ImageConvert is an independent project built by a developer who wanted a clean, fast, no-nonsense converter that respects user privacy. The site is hosted on GitHub Pages and the entire tool runs client-side.
